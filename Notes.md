Ballot Marking Rule Change Gate detects voter-help edits that change marking, correction, assistance, return, or privacy instructions. The authority seals exact bytes; a publisher submits bounded text; GenLayer validators compare those five fields plus jurisdiction, election, and version. The contract derives `PUBLISH_CURRENT`, `HOLD_STALE_RULE`, or `MANUAL_AUTHORITY_CHECK`, and writes no result for malformed extraction or validator disagreement.
