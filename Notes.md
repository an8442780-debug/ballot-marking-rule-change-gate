Ballot Marking Rule Change Gate protects a specific failure point in election publishing: a help-page edit can silently change how a voter marks, corrects, requests assistance, returns, or protects a ballot. An authority seals exact source bytes; an authorized publisher submits a bounded revision; GenLayer validators independently extract and compare the five action fields plus jurisdiction, election, and version metadata. The contract derives `PUBLISH_CURRENT`, `HOLD_STALE_RULE`, or `MANUAL_AUTHORITY_CHECK` from the accepted vector and writes no result when extraction is malformed or validators disagree. The deployed Studionet primitive is `0x1751Efa29Aaa0E4BaDF3a3f0e5d4E69a31878d10`; equal-vector S01b finalized with `PUBLISH_CURRENT`, while assistance-difference S04b finalized with `HOLD_STALE_RULE` ([S01b](https://explorer-studio.genlayer.com/tx/0x1a6f76322c6e4331105f0748096642e65368c21bf5fc29882f447394fe643f02), [S04b](https://explorer-studio.genlayer.com/tx/0x8d8ed9e50453db83e7f543112b968e5a88232d81494a98ed69398b612d8e8a8c)). Integrators can gate publication with `read_status`, show the compared fields with `read_action_vectors`, and verify provenance/lifecycle with `read_rule`. It never decides eligibility, candidates, winners, turnout, or legal compliance.
